Notes

D2 and D3 are adjacent and were returned as separate polygons (5.23 ha and 9.81 ha) sharing an edge with zero overlap.

The prediction layer is 2025 while the surveyed blocks are Spring 2026, so part of the residual difference is a real change in block layout between seasons, not model error.

D3's predicted 9.81 ha sits closer to the 11 ha the agronomy table gives for the D3 field than to the 8.18 ha surveyed block — the prediction may be mapping the field where the KMZ maps a block.

Edge offset is symmetric-difference area divided by surveyed perimeter: mean boundary displacement in metres. It is scale-free, unlike IoU, which penalises small blocks far harder than large paddocks.
